# Slide animation

Showing and hiding slide the whole drawer in from, and back out toward, the anchored screen edge over about 150 ms - pure translation, deliberately cheap for the compositor.

The drawer is everything you see - terminal, tab bar when one is shown, grab bar, its 1 px frame - sliding as one window over the desktop: the surface is a transparent canvas the size of the resting drawer, and the drawer carries all of its own paint, so the vacated area shows through while it travels. The move is a cubic ease-out curve on the way in, ease-in on the way out, pure translation with no fade. Toggle it with `slide_animation_enabled` (default `true`, the Dropdown page of the [Settings window](settings.md)) - `false` restores instant show/hide; either way the animation is deliberately cheap: the surface's geometry never changes during the animation, only the content's painted offset does, so the compositor sees no resize traffic. A toggle arriving mid-animation reverses the slide from wherever the content stands - nothing teleports, nothing is dropped or queued - and PTYs keep running throughout: keyboard focus stays with the drawer for the whole slide and is released at the completion destroy, the surface dying with it. A hide that catches the surface still measuring (a toggle within the show's measuring window) skips the animation and destroys immediately.
